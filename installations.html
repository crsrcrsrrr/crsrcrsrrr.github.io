48y51r15
- a gaze in to the void
48y51r15 is a sonic-visual work exploring the intangibility of sound and self regulation system.
Based on the practice of no-input mixing, the installation creates the linked dialogues between two domains of fluctuations.
The installation invites the perticipants to adjust observe and find the position/space in between.

Past installed venues ::
Bangkok Design Week 2025
Intact Festival 2024
